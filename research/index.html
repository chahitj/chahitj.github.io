Research

I study vision-language-action policies for robot manipulation, including how they respond to changes in objects, instructions, and scene layout. My Anchor-Align work focused on physical xArm7 evaluation and diagnosing rollout failures.

Research projects

arXiv preprint · ICLR 2027 submission in preparation
