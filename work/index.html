Anchor-Align

Anchor-Align studies how VLA finetuning can weaken visual-language grounding. I worked on the real-world xArm7 evaluation, including data collection, policy deployment, camera setup, and failure analysis.

WaypointGen

WaypointGen explored language-conditioned navigation in agricultural environments. I built the global planning pipeline, including crop-row structure, spatial graphs, and language-to-waypoint reasoning.

Long-Horizon Manipulation
